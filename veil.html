The relay only sees encrypted blobs, a random topic name, your IP and timing. Self-host ntfy for full control.
Honest security notes.
  • Messages are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The room code is turned into a key on your device.
  • The relay cannot read your messages, but it can see your IP, timing and ciphertext sizes.
  • Not forward-secret. If the room code leaks later and someone recorded the ciphertext, they can read past messages.
  • Not anonymous. The relay sees your network address.
  • There is no server-side history. Messages live only on the devices currently in the room (and in optional encrypted local storage).
